Digital Resource Kit User Manual

Weather in Rhythm: A Digital Songs and Poems Resource Kit for Year 4 ESL

1.0 Kit Overview

Weather in Rhythm is a web-based digital resource kit tailored specifically for Year 4 Primary ESL pupils. It promotes active, pupil-centred learning by combining musical rhythm, motor mimicry through Total Physical Response (TPR), classic literature appreciation via Robert Louis Stevenson’s poem "Rain", and creative expression through four-line poem writing paired with digital drawing.

2.0 Lesson Architecture & Time Allocation (60 Minutes)

Activity 1 (15 Mins) Move with the Weather!

• Pupils explore 4 core weather vocabulary words.
• Interact with 3D flashcards by flipping them to reveal example sentences.
• Sing along to the rhythm while performing TPR actions.
• Complete interactive 3D weather riddle challenges.

Focus: Listening & Speaking
Activity 2 (20 Mins) Weather Word Hunt

• Shared reading of Stevenson's classic poem "Rain" with gentle speech synthesis narration.
• Click highlighted words to inspect contextual definitions and pronunciations.
• Complete the /iː/ rhyming phoneme pair match challenge (tree – sea).

Focus: Reading & Phonics
Activity 3 (20 Mins) Draw It, Write It! Studio

• Select vocabulary prompts from the interactive Word Bank.
• Compose a personalized 4-line weather poem.
• Draw illustrations using digital crayons or in fullscreen mode.
• Export and download a high-resolution Little Poet's Certificate (PNG).

Focus: Writing & Creating

* The remaining 5 minutes are designated for Closure, Exit Ticket evaluation, and moral reflections.

3.0 Interactive Features Operational Guide

3.1 Background Music Controller (BGM)

Located on the top navigation bar. Click the BGM toggle button to initiate soft background arpeggio music for classroom ambience. Adjust the output level smoothly between 0% and 100% using the inline volume slider.

3.2 Speech Synthesis & 3D Flashcards (Activity 1)

Click any weather flashcard to trigger a 3D flip animation. The back of each card displays full-sentence usage and physical movement cues. All audio buttons include a pause toggle, allowing the teacher to regulate pronunciation pacing cleanly.

3.3 Rhyme Match Challenge (Activity 2)

Pupils listen to the ending sound of the target word tree and select its rhyming counterpart from the choices on the right (sea). Correct selections trigger a celebratory badge and encouraging automated speech feedback.

3.4 Drawing Studio & Native PNG Certificate Export (Activity 3)

• Palette & Stroke Sizing: Pupils can select from crayon colors, stroke thicknesses (Fine, Normal, Bold), and an Eraser tool.
• Fullscreen Drawing Mode: Click the Fullscreen button for expanded drawing space, and click Save & Exit Fullscreen to preserve and sync the artwork back.
• Download Certificate: Clicking Save My Poem & Drawing (Download PNG) renders an offline high-resolution certificate directly in browser memory, bypassing local cross-origin restrictions.

4.0 Teacher Zone Settings

Teachers can access the Teacher Zone panel in the upper-right corner. This drawer houses the speech synthesis voice selector (tuned to a gentle cadence suitable for primary learners) alongside a direct shortcut link to this user manual.

5.0 Technical Requirements & Offline Operation

• Supported Browsers: Current versions of Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Mozilla Firefox.

• Offline Compatibility: This kit runs locally as a standalone file. All audio synthesis, interactive canvas mechanics, and certificate export operations function on the teacher's machine without requiring a high-speed internet connection during class.
